## 1.3 Cut-to-shape decals vs a full wrap

Straightforward once you look at the area.

| Option | Applied area, one van | All-in cost at €35/m² (see §1.5) | Suits your artwork? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cut-to-shape printed decals** — logo panel on each door + rear | ~0.25–0.6 m² total | **€10–25 of material and print**, but priced as a minimum job: **800–1,500 MDL** | **Yes** |
| Partial wrap — decals plus a printed side band | 3–6 m² | €105–210 material, job ~€150–350 | Only if you want the vehicle to read as a moving billboard |
| **Full wrap** | 15–25 m² | **€525–875 material and labour**, plus removal cost later | **No** |

**Recommendation: cut-to-shape decals.** A full wrap on a work van that visits muddy sites is
money spent on a surface that will be scratched, and it is a 20–35× multiple of the cost for a
logo that is legible either way.

**One thing to be aware of, because it changes the quote.** Your artwork is *"dark charcoal
ground with gold"*. That is not a cut-to-shape job in the cheap sense — the printer has to
print the charcoal panel and then contour-cut it. You pay by printed area. The genuinely cheap
alternative is **plotter-cut lettering**: the logo cut out of a single colour of gold or white
vinyl and applied straight onto the vehicle paint, no printing at all, typically **30–50%
cheaper** and with a longer service life because there is no ink to fade. You lose the charcoal
ground. On a white or light-coloured van, that trade is usually worth taking. On a dark vehicle,
keep the printed panel. Ask for both prices — the script does.

## 1.4 Cast vs calendered, lamination, and real lifespan in Moldova

Manufacturer figures, VERIFIED from ORAFOL's own datasheets (the brand Nano-Art states it uses):

| Film | Type | Thickness | Manufacturer's outdoor durability | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ORAJET 3651** | Polymeric **calendered** | 2.75 mil | **5 years** | [orafol.com — ORAJET 3651](https://www.orafol.com/en/americas/products/orajet-3651) + [technical datasheet PDF](https://www.orafol.com/products/americas/en/technical-data-sheet/orajet-3651-id2993-technical-data-sheet-americas-en.pdf) |
| **ORAJET 3951** | **Cast** | 2 mil | Up to **10 years** unprinted white; **6–8 years** printed and laminated with ORAGUARD 290 | [orafol.com — ORAJET 3951](https://www.orafol.com/en/europe/products/orajet-3951) |

Not listed above but sold everywhere and worth naming: **monomeric calendered** film (the
cheapest grade, typically 1–3 year rating). This is the "дешёвая рекламная плёнка" that
Prostyle-md explicitly refuses to work with — their listing says such film *"через полгода
выгорает, отклеивается и требует переделки"* (fades, peels and needs redoing within six months).
That is a Moldovan supplier making your argument for you.

**Derating for Moldovan winters — ESTIMATE, reasoning shown.** Manufacturer durability ratings
are for vertical exposure in central Europe. Chișinău runs roughly −15 °C in January to +35 °C in
July, with road salt and frequent winter washing. Vehicle graphics also sit at angles, not
vertical, and get pressure-washed. Derate by **1–2 years**:

| Build | Manufacturer rating | Realistic in Moldova | Cost over 6 years, one van |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monomeric calendered, unlaminated | 1–3 yrs | **~2 yrs** | 3 × 1,200 MDL = **3,600 MDL** |
| **Polymeric calendered (3651) + laminate** | 5 yrs | **4–5 yrs** | 1 × 1,800 MDL (+ 1 partial redo) ≈ **2,200 MDL** |
| Cast (3951) + ORAGUARD 290 | 6–8 yrs | **6–7 yrs** | 1 × 3,000 MDL = **3,000 MDL** |

**Recommendation: polymeric calendered plus laminate, and insist the laminate is quoted.**
Your decals go on **flat door panels**. Cast film's advantage is conformability over rivets,
corrugations and compound curves — you do not have those. Paying cast prices for a flat door is
paying for a property you will not use. Cast becomes correct only if you later wrap a
corrugated box van or a curved bonnet.

The laminate is the part people skip and the part that matters — it is what stops UV fade and
what lets the decal survive a brush wash. **A laminated polymeric decal outlives an unlaminated
cast one.**

### 1.8 File format — what they will actually ask for

| Shop | Formats they state | Source |
|---|---|---|
| TG Print | **PDF, CDR, AI, EPS**; raster ≥ 300 dpi; **CMYK, Fogra 39** profile; plotter contour cutting to any shape | [tgprint.md](https://tgprint.md/stickere-si-etichete), VERIFIED |
| MediaPlan | **PDF, TIFF, PSD, AI** "and other formats" | [mediaplan.md](https://mediaplan.md/), VERIFIED |

**Neither lists SVG.** Nor did any other Moldovan shop I checked. Two practical consequences:

1. **Export a print-ready PDF/X-4 or EPS from the SVG before you send anything.** Keep the SVG as
   your master; send PDF. If the shop runs CorelDRAW — most Moldovan wide-format shops do — offer
   a `.cdr` as well.
2. **The CutContour layer.** Both quoted shops cut on a plotter and can read a contour, but the
   convention is a **spot colour named exactly `CutContour`, defined as 100% Magenta, set to
   overprint, on its own layer, with no fill.** Confirm they can read it (question 5 in the
   script). If they cannot, they will ask for a separate outline file — which is fine, just
   slower.
3. **Colour.** Your artwork is almost certainly RGB. Convert to CMYK (Fogra 39, per TG Print's
   spec) yourself and look at the result before sending, because **gold does not exist in
   four-colour process.** It prints as flat ochre or mustard. If the gold matters — and on a
   charcoal ground it is the whole design — either accept the flat version, or have the logo cut
   from **metallic gold vinyl** and applied over the printed charcoal panel. The second costs
   more and looks dramatically better. Raise it in the quote request.

### 1.9 One thing to check that nobody will tell you

**UNAVAILABLE — needs a phone call, not a web search.** Chișinău municipality operates an
outdoor-advertising authorisation regime, and the Fiscal Code (Titlul VII) provides for a local
tax on advertising devices. Branding on a company's **own** vehicle is normally treated as
identification rather than outdoor advertising, so no permit and no tax — **but I could not
verify this for Moldova and will not assert it.** Ask your accountant, or call the Direcția
generală comerț of the Chișinău Primărie before you commit to a large graphic. The cost of being
wrong is small; the cost of assuming is a fine.

## 2.5 Geo-targeting

Stăuceni sits on Chișinău's northern edge, which is an advantage: you are inside the city's

| Layer | Setting | Bid adjustment |
|---|---|---|

**The setting that will save you the most money, and that almost everyone gets wrong:** under
Location options, set **"Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations"** — *not*

serves your ads to Moldovans in Italy, Romania, Russia and the UK searching "constructii
Moldova". Some of them are genuinely building a house back home — most are reading news. On a
2,000 MDL budget the default will burn a third of it before you notice.

Weight it roughly **80% Chișinău radius / 20% rest of country**.

## 4.4 What to do first, and the order to add channels

**Weeks 1–2 — costs nothing but time**

1. **Claim the Google Business Profile.** Verification postcard takes 2–3 weeks; start the clock
   today. Everything else waits on it.
2. **Take the photographs.** Three completed projects, minimum. Phone photos with honest captions
   beat stock — the competitor analysis makes this case at length and it is correct. These photos
   feed the GBP, the site, Meta and 999.md simultaneously. **Highest-leverage two hours available
   to you.**
3. **Free listings** on construct.md, firme.md, lista.md, korter.md, kompass. One afternoon.

**Weeks 2–4 — under 1,500 MDL**

4. **999.md, set up properly.** Business account, RO and RU listings, colour highlight, scheduled
   auto-republish, Booster switched on with a small daily cap. At 0.20 MDL/click you find out
   what people actually click on for the price of a coffee.
5. **Send the vehicle-branding quote requests** (§1.7) to all four shops. Take the middle quote,
   not the cheapest — the cheapest is monomeric film and you will pay for it twice.

**Month 2 — the one that unlocks everything else**

6. **Fix the site.** Steps 1–5 of the competitor doc's execution order are pure deletion and can
   land in a day. Then: sticky phone + mobile call bar, `Cum lucrăm` process section, guarantee
   with a number, price anchor. **Do this before spending money on Google Ads**, because the
   conversion assumption in §2.3 depends on it.

**Month 2–3**

7. **Google Ads, minimum tier.** High-intent exact match, Chișinău radius, presence-only
   targeting, RU + RO. 1,000–2,000 MDL. Let it run 60 days before judging it.
8. **RO/RU switch** on the site. Largest addressable-market change available, and the data in the
   executive summary says so.

**Month 4–6 — only once the above is producing**

9. **Scale Google Ads to the moderate tier**, weighted by season (§2.6).
10. **Add Meta retargeting.** Cheap, and it works on people who already know who you are.
11. **Start weekly site-progress posts** — GBP, Facebook and the site, same content, three
    surfaces.

**Month 9+ — only if revenue justifies it**

12. **Cast-vinyl branding on any new vehicles**; SEO retainer if organic is growing but stalling;
    a billboard **only** on a road past an active site; agency management to buy your time back.

**Never, at this company size:** local TV, radio brand campaigns, Performance Max before 30
conversions a month, Display, or official Telegram Ads.
